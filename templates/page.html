{% if kind == 'about' %}

The mission of Sarana International Buddhist Center is to share the Buddha's message with everyone, bringing peace and happiness within ourselves and the world around us.

Located in Tukwila, Washington, between Seattle and Tacoma, SIBC welcomes people from all religions, traditions and cultures.

Spiritual practice and community life meet here: observance of precepts, Seth Pirith, Dhamma discussions and opportunities to meet monks and friends.
