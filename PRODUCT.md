# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose and people

Sarana International Buddhist Center in Tukwila welcomes people regardless of
religion, tradition or culture. The website helps community members learn about
the temple, find its address and existing calendar, contact SIBC and support it.
Existing public content, history and useful URLs are the factual source.

## Confirmed scope

Responsive, accessible core pages; English primary; Sinhala and Khmer draft
alternatives requiring human review; preserved historical content; source-linked
public imagery awaiting permission review. Stripe-hosted one-time/monthly/custom
donations use separate SIBC configuration, server-side secrets and verified,
duplicate-safe webhooks. Retain PayPal. Never enable live payments in this build.

No AI assistant, new event features, visitor guide, Dhamma School features, FAQ
system, announcement management, account creation or deployment.

## Evidence and open approvals

The approved Library inventory reports 131 public sitemap URLs. The repository
started with only a README. The existing website supplies mission, address,
activity descriptions, archives and public media, not a verified current schedule.
SIBC must review contact/schedules, financial claims, image permissions, translations
and payment ownership/operations before launch. Do not invent missing information.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic server-rendered pages, keyboard navigation,
visible focus, mobile reflow and honest translated-content status.
